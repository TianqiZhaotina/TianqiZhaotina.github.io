ABOUT ME

I work across research, learning design, and teaching.

My current work sits at the intersection of AI education, curriculum design, and the learning sciences. I'm especially curious about what students actually learn when they create with AI—and what their prompts, plans, tests, revisions, and explanations can tell us about that process.

Much of my work is collaborative. I co-design materials with educators, support professional learning, study how students and teachers use AI tools, and turn their feedback into the next version of a learning experience.
