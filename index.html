ABOUT ME

Designing AI-supported learning environments for student agency.

My research focuses on cultivating meaningful student agency in K–12 project-based STEM and computing. Drawing on self-regulated learning, I examine how students initiate, negotiate, share, delegate, and reclaim decision-making authority as they work with AI.

I connect this research with curriculum and learning-environment design. My longer-term goal is to develop design principles that help students use AI strategically while becoming more self-directed and intellectually independent learners.
