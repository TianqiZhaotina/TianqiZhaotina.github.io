CURRENT · RESEARCHER & INSTRUCTIONAL DESIGNER

Agentic Coding Studio

A six-week after-school program in which grades 7–12 learners design games with AI coding agents. The project asks how young people can use an agent’s capabilities while continuing to set goals, make design decisions, test ideas, debug, and explain their own work.

My contribution. I am developing the curriculum framework, learning objectives, 12-session lesson map, student activities, research questions, study design, and IRB materials. Each learner moves from modifying existing games to independently designing a game, with design plans, testing records, and reflections making the learning process visible.

Grades 7–12 · 6 weeks · 12 sessions · 90 minutes per session

CURRENT · EARLY CONCEPTUAL & INSTRUCTIONAL DESIGN

Science Inquiry Studio

An AI-supported environment for science inquiry. The design centers on students investigating, modeling, explaining, and revising—not simply receiving an answer from AI.

My contribution. I contributed to early design principles, professional learning activities, teacher discussion prompts, workshop support, and emerging research questions. Research question formulation with AI

Learning Analytics Lab · Teachers College, Columbia University

This study examined how novice learning analytics researchers formulate and refine research questions with language models. Rather than treating a better final prompt as the whole story, I studied how people iterate, check constraints, operationalize constructs, question AI suggestions, and recover from methodological problems.

EXPERIMENT & METHODOLOGY

Following both the product and the process

The product rubric examined specificity, data–method alignment, theoretical depth, and inquiry coherence. The process rubric examined prompt iteration, critical evaluation, help-seeking, and challenge resolution.

  1. 01
    Study design

    An IRB-approved, within-subjects think-aloud study with six master’s students. Each participant worked through two learning analytics scenarios with two language models, producing 24 sessions in total.

  2. 02
    Learning task

    Participants developed a Research Inquiry Cluster: one primary research question, two sub-questions, and a short methodological rationale grounded in the data available in each scenario.

  3. 03
    Evidence collected

    Chat histories, think-aloud data, final inquiry clusters, and reflections captured both the quality of the final research design and the interaction process that produced it.

  4. 04
    Analysis

    I combined product and process rubrics with hybrid deductive–inductive coding. The interaction data were segmented into 129 goal-centered episodes and reviewed using documented coding decisions.

RESEARCH OUTPUT

A practice-based roadmap

By connecting final inquiry quality with the interaction paths that produced it, the study develops practical guidance for research methods training: how to make constraints explicit, translate abstract constructs into observable indicators, verify method–data fit, and retain researcher judgment when AI proposes an attractive but infeasible direction.

First-authored AERA 2027 individual paper proposal: Human–AI Co-thinking in Research Question Formulation as Methodological Apprenticeship · under review.

UNDERGRADUATE THESIS · 2023–2024

Teaching Excellence Framework and lecturer practice

University of Manchester

A qualitative study of how university lecturers interpreted the Teaching Excellence Framework and whether institutional accountability measures shaped day-to-day teaching. I conducted semi-structured interviews with five lecturers across Education, Climate Change, Circular Economy, and Fashion Management, then coded the transcripts into four themes.

The study found limited direct influence on classroom practice, mixed views on feedback-driven improvement, and concerns about metric accountability, student-evaluation bias, and the marketization of teaching quality.
